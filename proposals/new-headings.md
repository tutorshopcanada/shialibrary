# Proposed new headings

Suggestions from cataloging sessions. Nothing here is in `data/vocabulary.yaml` until the owner approves it.

## 2026-11-10: thaqalayn.net pilot

- **id:** `legal-hadith`
  - **label:** "Hadith on legal topics (narrations about worship and practical law)"
  - **suggested parent:** `hadith` (NOT under `fiqh`, so these stay hadith records and do not trip the fiqh rule)
  - **why:** Several hadith volumes on the site are about legal subjects (purity, prayer, zakat and so on). They are primary-source narrations, not rulings, so they cannot take a Fiqh heading, and at the moment they can only be tagged `hadith-collections`, which makes them hard to find by subject.
  - **needed by:** `thaqalayn-al-kafi-vol-3` now; later Al-Kāfi vols 4-7, Man Lā Yaḥḍuruh al-Faqīh and Tahdhīb al-Aḥkām volumes if cataloged.

- **id:** `intellect-knowledge`
  - **label:** "Intellect and seeking knowledge"
  - **suggested parent:** `ethics` (or `beliefs`; owner to choose)
  - **why:** Al-Kāfi vol. 1 opens with books on intelligence and ignorance and on the virtue of knowledge; no existing heading covers this.
  - **needed by:** `thaqalayn-al-kafi-vol-1`


> 2026-11-10: `legal-hadith` was added to the vocabulary following the owner's decision that hadith always carry the Hadith label. `intellect-knowledge` is still only a proposal.
